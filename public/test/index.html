Help us test Plumb

We're recruiting roughly 50 side sleepers to try the first production sample of Plumb for about a month and tell us, honestly, what works and what doesn't. There's no charge for the unit and no obligation to keep using it; we just need clear, written feedback on a short questionnaire at the end.

Who we're looking for

  • You sleep on your side most nights.
  • You currently use, or have tried, a pillow between your knees.
  • You're between roughly 5'2" and 6'4" with a typical mid-thigh circumference of about 17–28 inches (we'll measure you in the screener).
  • You're in the continental US (shipping logistics).

Who we can't include right now

Pregnancy; recent leg, hip, or knee surgery; active lymphedema; a personal or family history of DVT. This isn't because Plumb is dangerous — it's because the wear-test period is exactly when we're learning what fit issues look like, and any of those conditions deserves a more careful design conversation than a Stage-1 test allows.

What we ask of you

  • Wear the sleeve and use the pillow most nights for four weeks.
  • Answer three short check-in emails (about a minute each).
  • Complete one longer questionnaire at the end (about ten minutes).
