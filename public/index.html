How Plumb works

A soft knit thigh sleeve with an open-ended elastic pocket on its inner side. The foam pillow slides into the pocket; a washable pillowcase goes over the foam. When you turn, everything turns with you. There is nothing to reattach in the dark.

  1. 1

    Put it on

    A soft tubular knit (viscose made from bamboo), about five inches tall, on the upper thigh of your top-resting leg. The foam pillow is already seated in the pocket, under the washable pillowcase.

  2. 2

    Sleep

    Roll, twist, kick. The sleeve grips the thigh and the pocket carries the pillow with it. The pillow does not slip out from between your knees because the leg the pocket is on is going wherever you are going.

  3. 3

    Wake up where you started

    Pillow still in place. No reaching for it on the floor. No towel between your knees as backup. No 3 a.m. adjustment.

Four failures. One design.

We coded 4,962 knee-pillow reviews against a unified taxonomy to find out exactly what fails for whom. The list below is the answer, in the order buyers complained about it. Plumb is designed against that list, in that order.

Stays put.

The pillow on the floor by 2 a.m. is the #1 complaint in every brand we coded. Plumb's foam pillow slides into an elastic pocket on a thigh sleeve. The sleeve moves with the leg that moves. The pillow goes where the leg goes. That is the whole design.

Sized for you, not for an average.

"Too short for tall people." "Too small for big thighs." "Too contoured for slim ones." Two sleeve sizes — Regular up to 28 inches, XL 28–32 inches — thigh-circumference fit, cut against U.S. anthropometric data. Not a one-size guess.

The right firmness for your thighs.

Most knee pillows are spec'd to feel nice in a showroom. Buyers call them "a block of wood" or say they "go flat in a week." Plumb's foam is specified for the load your thighs actually put on it. The foam is a replaceable insert, so when it wears, you swap the insert, not the product. See the load math →

The parts that touch you, wash.

Every other knee pillow is one foam block you cannot wash. Plumb is three pieces — the thigh sleeve, the foam pillow, and a washable pillowcase over the foam. The sleeve and the pillowcase go in the machine. The foam airs out and is replaced as a consumable when it wears.

Three pieces. One job.

A thigh sleeve. A foam pillow. A washable pillowcase. The pieces do different jobs, and each one is replaceable on its own when it needs to be.

The thigh sleeve

A soft tubular knit (viscose made from bamboo) worn on the upper thigh, with an open-ended elastic pocket on its body-side surface. Silicone dot grippers on the inner top rim hold the sleeve in place without binding. The pocket holds the foam pillow against your inner thigh through any sleep position you take, and releases one-handed when you want to take it off.

The foam pillow

A contoured hourglass foam core, CertiPUR-US polyurethane. Sized to match the sleeve. Two thicknesses. The foam is the consumable — when it eventually compresses, you swap the foam, not the whole product.

The washable pillowcase

A knit cover that slides over the foam pillow, between the foam and the elastic pocket. Removable. Machine washable on cold, tumble dry low. The sleeve also washes. The two parts that touch you both come off and go in the wash; the foam stays clean inside.

Honest answers

Will it actually stay in place?

That is the entire design. The sleeve grips the upper thigh; the foam pillow slides into an open-ended elastic pocket on the body-side of the sleeve; a washable pillowcase goes over the foam between the foam and the pocket. The sleeve moves with the leg that moves, so the pillow goes where the leg goes.

Is this a medical device?

No. Plumb is a comfort and sleep-alignment product. It's not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any condition. If you have a medical issue affecting your back, hips, knees, or sleep, talk to your physician.

How is this different from a $35 knee pillow sleeve?

Padded sleeves on the market put the cushion inside the sleeve, against the inner thigh of the worn leg — squeezed against your own skin — and can't be washed because the foam is sewn in. Plumb's case sits on the body-side of the sleeve so the foam pillow stays in the inter-thigh gap rather than compressed against your thigh. The sleeve-and-case is one knit fabric system that machine-washes; the foam pillow is a separate consumable replaced when it wears.

Will it cut off circulation?

The sleeve is soft knit and stretches significantly — closer to the feel of a wide compression sock than a strap. The grip comes from silicone dots, not from tightness. If a tester reports any numbness, tingling, or skin marks, we redesign. That's a hard kill criterion.

What's the foam?

A contoured hourglass polyurethane foam core, CertiPUR-US certified. About twelve inches long, seven inches wide, five inches thick (baseline) or six inches (optional).

Is it washable?

Most knee pillows are one foam block you can never wash. Plumb is built so the parts that touch you wash, and the part that doesn't, doesn't. The sleeve-and-case is one knit fabric system that machine-washes cold and air-dries. The foam pillow inside is never washed — it airs out between uses and is replaced as a separate consumable when it eventually wears.

Does it sleep hot?

The sleeve-and-case is a viscose-made-from-bamboo knit, targeted at the breathability profile typical of viscose-blend sleepwear. Per FTC textile labeling guidance, the fiber is described as "viscose made from bamboo," not "bamboo" alone. Third-party thermal testing is planned before launch.

How do I pick a size?

Two sleeve sizes — Regular (up to 28 in. upper-thigh circumference) and XL (28–32 in.). Measure around the fullest part of your upper thigh, about three inches below the crease. If you're between sizes, size down. Pillow thickness: five inches for most adults, six inches for larger frames or firmer mattresses.

Does it work for pregnancy?

Many side sleepers find a knee pillow more useful during pregnancy, and Plumb is designed to stay in place better than a loose pillow. We don't make any pregnancy-specific health claims; talk to your provider about what's appropriate for you.
