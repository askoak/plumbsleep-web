How Plumb works

One soft knit garment worn on the upper thigh, with a fitted case sewn permanently to its inner side. The foam pillow nests in the case. When you turn, everything turns with you — there is nothing to reattach in the dark.

  1. 1

    Put it on

    A soft tubular knit (viscose made from bamboo), about five inches tall, on the upper thigh of your top-resting leg. The foam pillow is already seated in the case.

  2. 2

    Sleep

    Roll, twist, kick — the sleeve grips the thigh and the case carries the pillow with it. The pillow does not slip out from between your knees, because it cannot leave the case it is sewn into.

  3. 3

    Wake up where you started

    Pillow still in place. No reaching for it on the floor. No towel between your knees as backup. No 3 a.m. adjustment.

Why Plumb is different.

We coded 4,962 knee-pillow reviews against a unified taxonomy to find out exactly what fails for whom. The list below is the answer, in the order buyers complained about it. Plumb is designed against that list, in that order.

Still there in the morning.

The most common failure is also the most exhausting one: the pillow ends up on the floor by 2 a.m. and the knee that needed support spent the night without it. Plumb's foam pillow sits inside a case sewn to the sleeve. There is no separable attachment to fall apart, no strap to twist, no buckle to dig in. Wear-test results published before launch.

Sized for your body.

Sizing is the number-one complaint in every tier of the category we surveyed. Pillows are too short for tall frames, too small for larger thighs, too contoured for slimmer ones. Plumb runs two sleeve sizes — Regular up to 28 inches and XL 28–32 inches — cut against U.S. anthropometric data, not a one-size guess. The foam pillow comes in two thicknesses to pair with it.

Right firmness for inter-thigh load.

Most knee pillows are spec'd to feel nice in a showroom, not to carry the load your thighs actually put on them through the night. Buyers describe it two ways — "more like a block of wood" or "goes flat in a week." Plumb's foam is specified at the firmness range the load math calls for. See the load math →

The parts that touch you wash.

The sleeve-and-case is one knit fabric system; it goes in the machine. Cold wash, tumble dry low. The foam pillow inside is never washed — it airs out and is replaced as a separate consumable when it eventually wears. Most knee pillows are a single foam block that nothing washes. Plumb is built the other way.

One garment. One job.

The sleeve and the case are one fabric system. The foam pillow nests inside the case and is replaced as a consumable when it wears. There is no separate attachment to break, lose, or fight with in the dark.

The sleeve-and-case

A soft tubular knit (viscose made from bamboo) worn on the upper thigh, with a fitted case sewn permanently to its body-side surface. Silicone dot grippers on the inner top rim hold the sleeve in place without binding. The case holds the foam pillow against your inner thigh through any sleep position you take.

The foam pillow

A contoured hourglass foam core, CertiPUR-US polyurethane. Twelve inches long. Five-inch baseline thickness; six-inch optional. Slides into the case from a body-side flap closure. Replaced as a consumable when it eventually compresses or wears.

What it isn't

It isn't a strap. It isn't a wedge wrapped in a sleeve. It isn't a foam block you can't wash. It isn't a buckle, a clip, or a band that ends up twisted on top of your thigh by morning. The single-piece architecture is the design, not a manufacturing shortcut — it's what removes the attachment as a failure mode.

Honest answers

Will it actually stay in place?

That's the entire design. The sleeve grips the upper thigh; the foam pillow sits inside a case sewn permanently to the body-side of the sleeve. The foam cannot separate from the sleeve because they are one fabric system. We'll publish wear-test results before launch.

Is this a medical device?

No. Plumb is a comfort and sleep-alignment product. It's not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any condition. If you have a medical issue affecting your back, hips, knees, or sleep, talk to your physician.

How is this different from a $35 knee pillow sleeve?

Padded sleeves on the market put the cushion inside the sleeve, against the inner thigh of the worn leg — squeezed against your own skin — and can't be washed because the foam is sewn in. Plumb's case sits on the body-side of the sleeve so the foam pillow stays in the inter-thigh gap rather than compressed against your thigh. The sleeve-and-case is one knit fabric system that machine-washes; the foam pillow is a separate consumable replaced when it wears.

Will it cut off circulation?

The sleeve is soft knit and stretches significantly — closer to the feel of a wide compression sock than a strap. The grip comes from silicone dots, not from tightness. If a tester reports any numbness, tingling, or skin marks, we redesign. That's a hard kill criterion.

What's the foam?

A contoured hourglass polyurethane foam core, CertiPUR-US certified. About twelve inches long, seven inches wide, five inches thick (baseline) or six inches (optional).

Is it washable?

Most knee pillows are one foam block you can never wash. Plumb is built so the parts that touch you wash, and the part that doesn't, doesn't. The sleeve-and-case is one knit fabric system that machine-washes cold and air-dries. The foam pillow inside is never washed — it airs out between uses and is replaced as a separate consumable when it eventually wears.

Does it sleep hot?

The sleeve-and-case is a viscose-made-from-bamboo knit, targeted at the breathability profile typical of viscose-blend sleepwear. Per FTC textile labeling guidance, the fiber is described as "viscose made from bamboo," not "bamboo" alone. Third-party thermal testing is planned before launch.

How do I pick a size?

Two sleeve sizes — Regular (up to 28 in. upper-thigh circumference) and XL (28–32 in.). Measure around the fullest part of your upper thigh, about three inches below the crease. If you're between sizes, size down. Pillow thickness: five inches for most adults, six inches for larger frames or firmer mattresses.

Does it work for pregnancy?

Many side sleepers find a knee pillow more useful during pregnancy, and Plumb is designed to stay in place better than a loose pillow. We don't make any pregnancy-specific health claims; talk to your provider about what's appropriate for you.
