Plumb

Sleep, aligned.

A wearable thigh band for adult side sleepers — a soft bamboo cuff with a contoured cushion mounted on the outside, designed to seat in the gap between the thighs and stay there. Replaces loose knee pillows. One band, worn on the dominant leg, in three cushion shapes and two colorways. Currently pre-prototype. Sharing for feedback.

Why side sleepers abandon knee pillows

Falls out.

Loose pillows slip between the knees during normal sleep movement.

Runs hot.

Foam blocks trap heat at the inner thigh — the warmest contact zone.

Wakes the sleeper.

Repositioning interrupts sleep cycles, especially after midnight.

Feels medical.

Straps and braces invite a treatment mindset most people resist long-term.

What Plumb is

Plumb is a bamboo-blend cuff (at least 3 inches wide) worn above the knee on the dominant leg. A contoured cushion mounts to the band's exterior — its base is gently concave to wrap the band, and its outward face is gently convex to meet the opposite thigh. When the wearer rolls to their side, the cushion seats naturally in the gap between the thighs, with both legs gripping it laterally to hold it in position. Silicone dot grippers on the inside top and bottom of the band keep it from sliding down the leg. One band, one cushion, one leg. Designed to feel like premium sleepwear, not a medical brace.

Pebble is the universal entry shape — a smooth oval that cradles between the thighs at the lowest profile. Walkable for a brief trip out of bed.
Saddle is our recommended default — a softly waisted shape that hugs the adductors for a more locked-in feel.
Wing is the heavy-sleeper option — the Pebble silhouette with two subtle lateral wings that engage both thighs for maximum hold. Recommended for larger frames or soft mattresses.

Two colorways

Plumb launches in two colors: Midnight (a soft slate-blue) and Dune (a warm terracotta-tinted oatmeal). Both with a vivid purple PLUMB wordmark embroidered on the front.
