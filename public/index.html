How Plumb works

The sleeve is the anchor. It goes on your upper thigh. The covered pillow attaches to the sleeve, so when you turn over, the pillow turns with you.

  1. 1

    Wear the sleeve

    A soft tubular knit (viscose made from bamboo), about five inches tall, on the upper thigh of your top-resting leg.

  2. 2

    Attach the pillow

    The covered foam pillow connects to the sleeve at the inner-thigh contact zone. The wear test is selecting the attachment mechanism.

  3. 3

    Sleep aligned

    Roll, twist, kick — the pillow goes where the sleeve goes. No reaching at 3 a.m.

Three pieces. One job.

Most knee pillows are one foam block you can never wash. Plumb is built so the parts that touch you wash — and the part that doesn't, doesn't.

The sleeve

A soft knit band — viscose made from bamboo — worn on the upper thigh. Sized to stay put without binding. Silicone dot grippers on the inner top rim do the holding, not the tightness.

The cover

A removable washable shell over the foam. Machine wash cold. Tumble dry low. We're targeting three covers per starter kit so one is always clean.

The pillow

A contoured hourglass foam core, CertiPUR-US polyurethane. Twelve inches long. Five-inch baseline thickness; six-inch optional. Airs out between covers. Never washed. Replaced when it wears.

Who Plumb is for

  • Side sleepers who already use a knee pillow and lose it most nights.
  • Side sleepers who've tried a knee pillow and given up on it.
  • Anyone who wants to stay aligned through the night without rigging a regular pillow into place.
  • Travelers who want a knee pillow that doesn't migrate in unfamiliar beds.

Who Plumb is not for

  • Stomach sleepers — a knee pillow isn't the right tool.
  • Anyone looking for a medical device. Plumb is a comfort product, not a treatment.

Honest answers

Will it actually stay in place?

That's the entire design. The sleeve grips the upper thigh; the covered pillow attaches to the sleeve. When you turn, the pillow turns with you. We'll publish wear-test results before launch.

Is this a medical device?

No. Plumb is a comfort and sleep-alignment product. It's not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any condition. If you have a medical issue affecting your back, hips, knees, or sleep, talk to your physician.

How is this different from a $35 knee pillow sleeve?

Padded sleeves on the market put the cushion inside the sleeve, against the inner thigh of the worn leg — and can't be properly washed because the foam is sewn in. Plumb is three separate pieces: the sleeve, a removable washable cover, and a contoured foam pillow. The covered pillow attaches to the sleeve, so it stays in the inter-thigh gap rather than being squeezed against your own skin — and every piece can be washed or replaced independently.

Will it cut off circulation?

The sleeve is soft knit and stretches significantly — closer to the feel of a wide compression sock than a strap. The grip comes from silicone dots, not from tightness. If a tester reports any numbness, tingling, or skin marks, we redesign. That's a hard kill criterion.

What's the foam?

A contoured hourglass polyurethane foam core, CertiPUR-US certified. About twelve inches long, seven inches wide, five inches thick (baseline) or six inches (optional).

Is it washable?

Most knee pillows are one foam block you can never wash. Plumb is built so the parts that touch you wash, and the part that doesn't, doesn't. The sleeve and the cover machine-wash cold and air-dry. The foam pillow itself is never washed — it airs out between uses and is designed to be replaced as a separate part when it eventually wears.

Does it sleep hot?

The sleeve and cover are a viscose-made-from-bamboo knit, targeted at the breathability profile typical of viscose-blend sleepwear. Per FTC textile labeling guidance, the fiber is described as "viscose made from bamboo," not "bamboo" alone. Third-party thermal testing is planned before launch.

How do I pick a size?

Two sleeve sizes — Regular (up to 28 in. upper-thigh circumference) and XL (28–32 in.). Measure around the fullest part of your upper thigh, about three inches below the crease. If you're between sizes, size down. Pillow thickness: five inches for most adults, six inches for larger frames or firmer mattresses.

Does it work for pregnancy?

Many side sleepers find a knee pillow more useful during pregnancy, and Plumb is designed to stay in place better than a loose pillow. We don't make any pregnancy-specific health claims; talk to your provider about what's appropriate for you.

When can I buy one?

Not yet. Pre-prototype. If the wear test passes, we'll open a waitlist and ship the first production run later in 2026. Join the waitlist below and you'll be among the first to know.

What if I don't love it?

Plumb is being designed around a 90-night home trial with free returns both ways. Final trial terms will be published before launch, after wear testing and production economics are complete. If Plumb isn't the one for you, you should be able to send it back without friction.
