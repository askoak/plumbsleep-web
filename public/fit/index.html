A five-question fit check

Find your Plumb.

Five honest questions. Two minutes. The output is a personalized configuration with the corpus evidence behind each recommendation. No tracking, no third-party scripts, nothing stored unless you save your fit at the end.

Plumb's product design is built on 4,962 coded Amazon reviews of side-sleep pillows. The recommendations below cite the specific finding behind each one. See the methodology →

How do you sleep most nights?

Pick the position you spend the most hours in. If you change positions, pick what you start in.

What is your upper-thigh circumference?

Plumb makes two sleeve sizes. This question decides which one fits you. Measure the widest part of your thigh just below the hip joint if you have a tape measure; otherwise pick the closest estimate.

What goes wrong most often?

Pick as many as apply. These are the failures buyers describe most in the coded corpus.

What have you tried before?

Pick whatever you have used at least once. We will show you the specific complaints buyers had about each product, and what Plumb does differently.

How does your sleep temperature usually run?

This decides whether the cooling sleeve is doing real work for you or is incidental.
