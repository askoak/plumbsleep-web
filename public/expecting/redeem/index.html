Plumb's promise to moms

Redeem a free sleeve.

You're here because you have a Plumb thank-you card, or someone shared this with you. Here's how Plumb's promise to moms works — and how to get a free sleeve when redemption opens.

Which describes you?

I'm a planning or expecting mom, redeeming for myself.

When redemption opens, you'll fill out a short form, and we'll send you the Plumb Maternity sleeve. You pay only what shipping costs us at the carrier. The sleeve itself is free. The pillow and cover aren't included — the sleeve is the gift.

I'm a backer holding a card, and I'd like Plumb to route it to a mom.

Hold onto your card — you might meet someone who needs it. If you'd rather hand it back to us, that option opens after the campaign, when we route unredeemed cards to planning and expecting moms through a partner network. Nothing to do right now.

Redemption opens when the Kickstarter campaign closes. We're pre-launch right now, so there's nothing to redeem yet. Leave your email below and we'll tell you the moment the redemption form is live — nothing else.

We use your address only to tell you when redemption opens. We do not sell or share addresses. To delete yours, email hello@plumbsleep.com.
